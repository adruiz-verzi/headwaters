# Summit Venture Studio — what the Sept 19–22 research chat produced

SVS is a Verzi prospect and the second reader of the Uni Research engine (Verzi strategy is the first). Every compiled
page in the engine carries a "Verzi read" and an "SVS read": university teams and grant-funded early companies,
ranked by stage. This file is the SVS-facing summary of the two harvest runs and the pilot; the cited detail is in
`/Users/adruiz/projects/verzi/Uni Research Strategy/`.

## The stage ladder used on every team and company page

paper → grant → patent/disclosure → I-Corps → STTR / PFI-TT → SBIR Phase I → SBIR Phase II → spinout.
Grants are the earliest public signal of a university technology and the highest-signal, lowest-cost source
(NIH RePORTER, NSF awards; SBIR/STTR/PFI-TT/I-Corps flagged). Next-run signals are always "did a Phase II follow" and
"did the PI's next paper name a company."

## How to run a pass for SVS

In a terminal: `cd "/Users/adruiz/projects/verzi/Uni Research Strategy"`, export `OA_KEY` (and `GITHUB_TOKEN` for
the code tier), start `claude`, then "Run uni-research on <topic> with SVS as the second reader." Give it five or six
phrases practitioners in that field actually use. The run writes team and company pages with SVS reads, a digest, and
a log entry; copy the digest's SVS section here.
